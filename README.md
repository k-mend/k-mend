### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **B.Sc. Mathematics & Computer Science** student at **Kenyatta University**, passionate about solving real-world challenges in **Sustainability** and **Climate Adaptation** using Machine Learning.

My work focuses on the intersection of **Generative AI (RAG/Vision)** and **Cloud Infrastructure**. I don't just train models in notebooks; I deploy them to production using **AWS Lambda, Docker, and FastAPI**.

- 🚀 Currently building [**Breme**](https://breme.pages.dev), YouTube analytics for creators in Kenya, priced in shillings.
- 💡 I'm looking to collaborate on **Open Source Machine Learning projects**.
